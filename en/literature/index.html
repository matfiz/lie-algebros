Bibliography

Reading pointers from each lecture.

By lecture

These pointers are taken from the lecture sources.

Lecture 1 · Lie algebras, ideals and representations

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 1–2 and 6.1. Erdmann–Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras, Chapters 1–2 and 7. Exercise Sheet 1 gives further calculations and proofs.

Lecture 2 · Nilpotency and Engel’s theorem

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 3.2–3.3; Section 3.1 belongs with Lecture 3. Erdmann–Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras, Chapters 4–6. Exercise Sheet 2 treats centres, quotients and the limits of the matrix criterion.

Lecture 3 · Solvability and Lie’s theorem

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 3.1, 4.1 and 4.2. Erdmann–Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras, Chapters 4–6. Levi’s theorem: Fulton–Harris, Appendix E.1. Exercise Sheet 3 gives further calculations and proofs.

Lecture 4 · The Killing form and semisimple structure

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 4.2–4.3 and 5.1–5.4. Erdmann–Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras, Chapter 9. Exercise Sheet 4 treats orthogonal ideals, quotients and the Killing form of sln\sllie_nsln​.

Lecture 5 · Casimir operators and complete reducibility

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 6.1–6.3, and Section 4.3 for Cartan’s criterion. Erdmann–Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras, Appendix B. Exercise Sheet 5 computes further Casimir operators and commutants.

Lecture 6 · Representations of sl2\sllie_2sl2​

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Section 7. Erdmann–Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras, Chapter 8. Fulton–Harris, Representation Theory: A First Course, Lecture 11, §§11.1–11.2. Exercise Sheet 6 treats symmetric powers, invariant forms, singlets, tensor Casimir operators, further decompositions and infinite-dimensional strings.

Lecture 7 · Cartan subalgebras and root decomposition

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 8.1–8.3; the imports are from Sections 5.4, 6.4, 15.3 and 16.4. Erdmann–Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras, Chapter 10. Exercise Sheet 7 gives further calculations and proofs.

Lecture 8 · Root strings and rank-one subalgebras

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 8.3–8.5. Erdmann–Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras, Sections 10.4–10.6.

Lecture 9 · Root systems, Weyl groups and weight lattices

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 9–10 and 13.1–13.2. Kirillov, An Introduction to Lie Groups and Lie Algebras, Chapter 7. Exercise Sheet 9 compares B2B_2B2​ with C2C_2C2​ and works through chamber walls and the finiteness lemma.

Lecture 10 · Dynkin diagrams and the classification

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 11–12, 14 and 18–19. Erdmann–Wildon, Introduction to Lie Algebras, Chapters 12–14. Carter, Lie Algebras of Finite and Affine Type, Chapter 8.

Lecture 11 · Universal enveloping algebras and PBW

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 17.1–17.4; the independence proof follows Section 17.4. Hall, Lie Groups, Lie Algebras, and Representations, 2nd edition, Chapter 9.

Lecture 12 · Verma modules and irreducible highest weights

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Section 20 and Subsection 21.1. Kirillov, An Introduction to Lie Groups and Lie Algebras, Sections 8.2–8.3.

Lecture 13 · Finite-dimensional existence and classification

Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Section 21. Kirillov, An Introduction to Lie Groups and Lie Algebras, Sections 8.3 and 8.9. Carter, Lie Algebras of Finite and Affine Type, Section 10.3.

Lecture 14 · The category of finite-dimensional representations

A. Kirillov Jr., An Introduction to Lie Groups and Lie Algebras, Sections 4.2 and 8.1. P. Etingof, Lie Groups and Lie Algebras (arXiv:2201.09397), the sections on tensor products and dual representations. J. E. Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Section 10.3, for the imported transitivity of WWW on bases.

Lecture 15 · Formal characters and the Weyl denominator

J. E. Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Sections 22–24 and Section 10.3. B. Hall, Lie Groups, Lie Algebras, and Representations, second edition (2015), Sections 10.4 and 10.6–10.7.

Lecture 16 · The Weyl character formula

B. Hall, Lie Groups, Lie Algebras, and Representations, second edition (2015), Sections 10.7–10.8; Hall uses a convex-hull argument where we use dominance. J. E. Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, Section 24 and its appendix. R. Carter, Lie Algebras of Finite and Affine Type (2005), Chapter 12. Exercise Sheet 16 repeats the proof in rank one.

Lecture 17 · Dimensions and weight multiplicities

J. E. Humphreys, Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory, §22.3 (Freudenthal’s formula), §24.2 (Kostant’s multiplicity formula) and §24.3 (Weyl’s formulas). A. Kirillov Jr., An Introduction to Lie Groups and Lie Algebras (2008), §§8.5–8.6. B. Hall, Lie Groups, Lie Algebras, and Representations, second edition (2015), Chapter 10. Exercise Sheet 17 contains the type C2C_2C2​ calculations.

Lecture 18 · Type A: from Schur modules to highest weights

The finite-group course, Lectures 13–14. Fulton and Harris, Representation Theory: A First Course, Section 4.1, Sections 6.1–6.2 and Sections 15.2–15.3. Goodman and Wallach, Symmetry, Representations, and Invariants, Sections 9.1 and 9.3.

Lecture 19 · Type A tensor products

Fulton–Harris, §6.1, §15.3 and Appendix A.1, for Pieri and Littlewood–Richardson. Goodman–Wallach, §9.3. Sheet 19 gives further products and an invariant cubic.

Lecture 20 · Type A branching and Gelfand–Tsetlin patterns

The finite-group course, Lecture 14 and its optional sheet, for the interlacing identity and the tableau formula. William Fulton and Joe Harris, Representation Theory: A First Course, §15. Roe Goodman and Nolan R. Wallach, Symmetry, Representations, and Invariants, Chapter 8, especially §8.1 on branching for general linear groups. Exercise Sheet 20 gives further calculations.

Lecture 21 · Type C fundamental representations

William Fulton and Joe Harris, Representation Theory: A First Course, Lectures 16–17. Roger Carter, Lie Algebras of Finite and Affine Type, Sections 13.6–13.7. Exercise Sheet 21 gives further calculations.

Lecture 22 · Type C tensor constructions and calculations

William Fulton and Joe Harris, Representation Theory: A First Course, §17.3. Roe Goodman and Nolan R. Wallach, Symmetry, Representations, and Invariants, Chapter 10. Roger Carter, Lie Algebras of Finite and Affine Type, Chapter 13. Exercise Sheet 22 gives further calculations.

Lecture 23 · Types B and D tensor representations

W. Fulton and J. Harris, Representation Theory: A First Course, §§18–19. R. Carter, Lie Algebras of Finite and Affine Type, §§13.3–13.4. R. Goodman and N. R. Wallach, Symmetry, Representations, and Invariants, Chapter 10.

Lecture 24 · Clifford algebras and spin representations

W. Fulton and J. Harris, Representation Theory: A First Course, §20.1. R. Carter, Lie Algebras of Finite and Affine Type, §13.5. Goodman–Wallach, Symmetry, Representations, and Invariants, Chapter 6.

Lecture 25 · Spinor tensor products and triality

Fulton–Harris, §§20.1–20.3 (octonionic triality in §20.3). Goodman–Wallach, Chapter 6. Humphreys, §§18.3–18.4, for Serre’s theorem.

Lecture 26 · Orthogonal branching and classical comparisons

Goodman and Wallach, Symmetry, Representations, and Invariants (2009), Section 8.1.1, Theorems 8.1.3–8.1.5, pp. 364–365. Fulton and Harris, Representation Theory: A First Course, Lectures 18–20 on orthogonal Lie algebras and spin representations, and §25.3 on branching rules. Exercise Sheet 26 gives further restrictions and proofs.

Lecture 27 · Invariant tensors and commutants

Fulton–Harris, Appendix F.1, and Goodman–Wallach, §5.3, give the invariant theorems cited above. Kirillov, §8.6, treats multiplicities. Exercise Sheet 27 gives further calculations.

Lecture 28 · The centre of U(g)U(\g)U(g) and central characters

Humphreys, §§22–24; Kirillov, §8.8; Carter, Chapter 11. The isomorphism and linkage sources are specified in Section 28.4.
