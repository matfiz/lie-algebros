Lecture 19

Type A tensor products

A tensor-product multiplicity is a coefficient in a product of characters. In type A the characters are Schur polynomials. Their products are computed by adding boxes to Young diagrams.

Throughout, g\gg is a complex semisimple Lie algebra with the data fixed in Lecture 9. All modules are finite-dimensional. For type AAA, assume n≥2n\ge2n≥2. Write μ≤λ\mu\le\lambdaμ≤λ when λ−μ\lambda-\muλ−μ is a sum of simple roots with nonnegative integer coefficients.

Pieri’s rule multiplies by a single row. A general μ\muμ requires tableaux. Draw a partition ν\nuν as a Young diagram with νi\nu_iνi​ boxes in row iii, rows numbered downwards.

Definition 19.11 (Littlewood–Richardson tableau). Let λ⊆ν\lambda\subseteq\nuλ⊆ν and μ\muμ be partitions. A Littlewood–Richardson tableau of shape ν/λ\nu/\lambdaν/λ and content μ\muμ is a filling of the boxes of ν\nuν not in λ\lambdaλ by positive integers such that:

  1. entries weakly increase from left to right along each row;

  2. entries strictly increase from top to bottom down each column;

  3. exactly μj\mu_jμj​ entries equal jjj, for each jjj;

  4. the reverse reading word, read from right to left along each row, from the top row to the bottom row, is a lattice word: every initial segment contains at least as many entries jjj as entries j+1j+1j+1, for every j≥1j\ge1j≥1.

Theorem 19.12 (Littlewood–Richardson rulecited). Fulton–Harris, Appendix A, Section A.1. Let λ,μ,ν\lambda,\mu,\nuλ,μ,ν be partitions with at most nnn parts, and define cλμνc^\nu_{\lambda\mu}cλμν​ by (19.1). Then cλμνc^\nu_{\lambda\mu}cλμν​ is the number of Littlewood–Richardson tableaux of shape ν/λ\nu/\lambdaν/λ and content μ\muμ. It is 000 unless λ⊆ν\lambda\subseteq\nuλ⊆ν. It is the same for every n≥max⁡(ℓ(λ),ℓ(μ),ℓ(ν))n\ge\max\bigl(\ell(\lambda),\ell(\mu),\ell(\nu)\bigr)n≥max(ℓ(λ),ℓ(μ),ℓ(ν)).

Put λ(0)=λ\lambda^{(0)}=\lambdaλ(0)=λ. For each j≥1j\ge1j≥1, the boxes of λ\lambdaλ together with the boxes with entries ≤j\le j≤j form a partition shape λ(j)\lambda^{(j)}λ(j). The boxes with entry jjj form the horizontal strip λ(j)/λ(j−1)\lambda^{(j)}/\lambda^{(j-1)}λ(j)/λ(j−1). The rule is applied as follows.

  1. Add μ1\mu_1μ1​ boxes labelled 111 to λ\lambdaλ as a horizontal strip.

  2. Add μ2\mu_2μ2​ boxes labelled 222 to the new shape as a horizontal strip, then μ3\mu_3μ3​ boxes labelled 333, and so on.

  3. Keep the fillings whose reverse reading word is a lattice word.

For μ=(k)\mu=(k)μ=(k) every entry is 111. The lattice condition then holds automatically, and the rule reduces to Pieri’s rule.

Summary

Tensor multiplicities are dimensions of singular subspaces. Highest-weight subtraction extracts them from characters.

In type A the multiplicities are Littlewood–Richardson coefficients, read after determinant shifts. Pieri’s rule adds horizontal strips. For sl3\sllie_3sl3​, 8⊗88\otimes88⊗8 contains the adjoint module once in each symmetry type.
