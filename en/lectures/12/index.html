Summary

PBW makes M(λ)M(\lambda)M(λ) free over U(n−)U(\mathfrak{n}_-)U(n−​), with weight spaces of dimension P(ν)\mathcal{P}(\nu)P(ν) and a one-dimensional top weight space.
