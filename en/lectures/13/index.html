13.4 The classification

Theorem 13.10 (ClassificationP). The assignment λ↦L(λ)\lambda\mapsto L(\lambda)λ↦L(λ) induces a bijection from P+P_+P+​ to the set of isomorphism classes of finite-dimensional simple g\gg-modules.

Surjective. By Lecture 12, every finite-dimensional simple module is isomorphic to L(λ)L(\lambda)L(λ) for some λ∈P+\lambda\in P_+λ∈P+​.

Summary

For λ∈P+\lambda\in P_+λ∈P+​, the finite-dimensional simple module L(λ)L(\lambda)L(λ) is the quotient of M(λ)M(\lambda)M(λ) by the submodule generated by fimi+1vλf_i^{m_i+1}v_\lambdafimi​+1​vλ​.
