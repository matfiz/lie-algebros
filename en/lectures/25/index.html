Spinor tensor products and triality

What this lecture does

Endomorphisms of spinors form a Clifford algebra, or its even part in odd dimension. As an orthogonal module this is a sum of exterior powers. In type DDD, chirality and duality decide which exterior powers occur.

Summary

The algebra End⁡(S)\End(S)End(S) is the Clifford algebra, or its even part in odd dimension. Hence S⊗S∗S\otimes S^*S⊗S∗ is a sum of exterior powers.

In D4D_4D4​, triality permutes 8v8_v8v​, 8s8_s8s​ and 8c8_c8c​.
