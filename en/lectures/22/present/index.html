Summary

Symmetric powers of VVV are irreducible: Sym⁡kV≅L(kω1)\operatorname{Sym}^kV\cong L(k\omega_1)SymkV≅L(kω1​). In general, the harmonic Schur module intersects SλV\mathbb{S}_\lambda VSλ​V with the kernels of all pair contractions. The symplectic Weyl construction identifies it with L(λ)L(\lambda)L(λ) when λ\lambdaλ has at most nnn parts, and gives zero otherwise.

A tensor product of fundamental modules contains L(λ)L(\lambda)L(λ) once, where λ\lambdaλ is the sum of their highest weights. The tensor product of highest vectors generates this summand.

For sp4\mathfrak{sp}_4sp4​, characters and contractions decompose small products. A repeated summand has an explicit multiplicity space, such as Hom⁡g(V,V⊗3)\Hom_\g(V,V^{\otimes3})Homg​(V,V⊗3).
