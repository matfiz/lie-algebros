The Weyl character formula

What this lecture does

A Verma character is an infinite series. Let λ∈P+\lambda\in P_+λ∈P+​. We prove that the product of the Weyl denominator and the character of L(λ)L(\lambda)L(λ) is a single finite alternant. The Casimir element puts the exponents of this product on a sphere. Dominance then leaves one Weyl orbit.

Summary

The Casimir element acts on a highest-weight module of highest weight μ\muμ by ∥μ+ρ∥2−∥ρ∥2\lVert\mu+\rho\rVert^2-\lVert\rho\rVert^2∥μ+ρ∥2−∥ρ∥2.
