What this lecture does

Summary

Intertwiners are invariant tensors. Multiplicity spaces determine commutants. Classical invariant tensors are built from pairings and volume tensors.

Characters identify K(g)K(\g)K(g) with Z[P]W\mathbb Z[P]^WZ[P]W, a polynomial ring in the fundamental characters. Lecture 28 turns to the centre of U(g)U(\g)U(g).
