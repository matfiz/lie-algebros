The problems use complex finite-dimensional spaces. Casimir operators are formed with the trace form of the module, as in Lecture 5. Exercises 5.1–5.3 compute Casimir operators and compare forms. Exercise 5.4 treats isotypic multiplicities and commutants. Exercises 5.5–5.6 locate the failure of complete reducibility outside the semisimple case.
