What this lecture does

Summary

Newton’s identities show that the operators ρd(X)\rho_d(X)ρd​(X) generate the commutant End⁡Sd(Td)\End_{S_d}(T^d)EndSd​​(Td). Hence, for ℓ(λ)≤n\ell(\lambda)\le nℓ(λ)≤n, the module Sλ(Cn)\mathbb S_\lambda(\C^n)Sλ​(Cn) is irreducible over gln\gl_ngln​. It stays irreducible over sln\sllie_nsln​ because III acts by the scalar ∣λ∣|\lambda|∣λ∣.

The bialternant is the Weyl character formula in coordinates. It identifies Sλ(Cn)\mathbb S_\lambda(\C^n)Sλ​(Cn) with L(λˉ)L(\bar\lambda)L(λˉ), with labels λi−λi+1\lambda_i-\lambda_{i+1}λi​−λi+1​. Normalised partitions give every simple sln\sllie_nsln​-module. Adding a full column tensors the gln\gl_ngln​-module with det⁡\detdet.
