Lecture 9

Root systems, Weyl groups and weight lattices

The roots of a semisimple Lie algebra satisfy a few axioms about reflections. We study these axioms abstractly. A choice of positive roots gives simple roots, a fundamental chamber and an order on weights. Simple reflections move every vector into the closure of that chamber.

Summary

A choice of positive roots gives simple roots. They form a basis in which every root has integer coordinates of one sign. Simple reflections permute the other positive roots, shift ρ\rhoρ by a simple root, and generate WWW.

A vector lies in the weight lattice exactly when its pairings with the simple coroots are integers. Every Weyl orbit in PPP meets P+P_+P+​. By the cited chamber theorem, it meets P+P_+P+​ exactly once. Only finitely many dominant weights lie below a dominant weight. Lecture 13 uses this finiteness to bound the weights of a highest-weight quotient.
