Source: Humphreys §§14.2 and 18.3–18.4. His printed matrix is transposed.

Root-system classification uses Euclidean geometry. Serre’s theorem supplies existence and uniqueness of the algebra with the prescribed Cartan matrix.

By a cited theorem [C: Humphreys §14.1], a semisimple algebra is simple exactly when its root system is irreducible. Hence each complex simple Lie algebra is isomorphic to the algebra of exactly one type in the table. For types AAA–DDD it is a matrix algebra of §10.2.

Summary

A base gives a Cartan matrix, and the diagram records the same data. Positivity of qΓq_\GammaqΓ​ is the test behind the classification, which restricts connected diagrams to types AAA–GGG. The classical algebras realise AAA–DDD. Serre’s theorem shows that the Cartan matrix determines the algebra. With the classification theorem, it shows that every type occurs.
