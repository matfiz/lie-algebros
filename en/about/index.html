About this course

lie algebras and their representation theory.

Course structure

Two parts of fourteen lectures. Part I develops structure and highest weights. Part II treats characters and explicit classical representations.

Prerequisites

Linear algebra, finite groups and their representations, and basic category language are assumed. Lie groups and physics courses are not prerequisites.

How to study

Reconstruct the calculations and proofs, attempt the self-checks, then work on the exercise sheet. The labels [P], [S] and [C] mean proved, sketched and cited.

The notes and exercises come from the same LaTeX sources as the PDFs. Numbering is checked against the typeset volume. Full exercise solutions belong to the separate course edition.
